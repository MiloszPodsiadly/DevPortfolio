# Privacy review checklist

This is a future review aid, not a legal opinion or confirmation that a privacy review has been completed.

- Recheck the actual hosting, logs, cookies, network requests, and third-party dependencies before launch.
- Keep analytics and tracking disabled unless the owner makes a separate decision after reviewing obligations.
- Do not add a contact form or collect visitor data without a new product and privacy review.
- If email or analytics are added later, update the public privacy information to match the real configuration.
- Review image, profile, and employment content for consent, licensing, and confidential details.
- Confirm the applicable privacy notice and legal obligations for the actual operator and audience before production.
